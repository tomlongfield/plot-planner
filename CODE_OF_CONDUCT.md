# Code of conduct

This project is for growers of every background and experience. We want taking part to be friendly and welcoming for everyone.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to growing or to open source.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Disagree about ideas, not people, and accept that growing practice varies by place and tradition.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact details on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/tomlongfield). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously do so.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
